## Title

## Phase

P5

## Objective

Write the abbreviation policy Andu approved on 2026-11-14 (Asia/Hong_Kong) into [Q-SCI-16](../docs/EVALUATION.md#q-sci-16) and close that question. Leave the workbench character-error report unbuilt.

## Problem

[P5-01](P5-01.md) and [P5-02](P5-02.md) are Done. The recognizer stores Unicode NFC `ocr_inferred` graphemes and writes no score. When this ticket opens, P5 is not complete. The remaining official exit criterion is a workbench character-error number. [Q-SCI-16](../docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#q-sci-16) is what has blocked that number: the abbreviation policy was not frozen. A policy chosen after scoring would change the metric. [ADR-0010](../docs/adr/0010-local-greek-htr-runtime.md) already records that abbreviation expansion can be unpredictable. Storage rules did not need a new schema.

## User or scientific outcome

A later ticket author can see the frozen policy: which lines stay out of the character-error set, that every other scored line is compared as written, and that storage stays Unicode NFC. This ticket leaves the scholar without a workbench character-error number. [P5-04](P5-04.md) later reports one on a synthetic fixture. The PP-OCRv6 medium model card's cited Ancient Greek CER 7.21% and WER 44.03% stay citations.
