## Title

Invalidate edges and citing joins when an outline revision is superseded.

## Phase

P3

## Objective

In about one day, drop a superseded revision's edges and dependent joins out of later ranks without deleting those rows.

## Problem

Superseding an outline appends `invalidated` on its edge arcs and on the join candidates that cite those edges. [P2-06](P2-06.md) stores the new outline and leaves edges untouched, because those rows did not exist yet. Leaving the old joins in the rank list would keep a superseded boundary in the order.

## User or scientific outcome

A benchmark operator can supersede an outline, keep the previous polygon and the join content, and see a later physical rank omit the joins that cited the old edges.

## Inputs

- The supersede sentence on `FragmentOutlineRevision` in [DATA_MODEL.md](../docs/DATA_MODEL.md#fragmentoutlinerevision).
- The supersede and correct paths from [P2-06](P2-06.md).
- `EdgeArc` and `JoinHypothesis` rows from [P3-02](P3-02.md), and the rank function from [P3-05](P3-05.md).

## Outputs

- The existing supersede and correct operations append `invalidated` on every `EdgeArc` of that revision and on every join candidate that cites one of those edge ids. The append happens in the same write as the new revision.
- The previous polygon, the edge geometry, and the join content stay. The rows are not deleted and are not rewritten.
- A revision with no edges appends no invalidation events.
- A later rank from [P3-05](P3-05.md) omits those joins.

## Epistemic-class impact

no. Invalidation is a `StatusEvent`. It does not re-type the outline, the edge, or the join, and it does not turn the new revision into observed evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Superseding a revision that owns edges appends `invalidated` on each of those edges and on each join that cites one of those edge ids.
- [x] The previous polygon, the edge geometry, and the join content are unchanged. A human correction still stores the new outline from [P2-06](P2-06.md).
- [x] A revision with no edges appends no invalidation event.
- [x] A join that cites an edge of a different revision is left on its previous status.
- [x] A later physical rank omits the invalidated joins and still includes a join that cites only current edges.
- [x] About one day. No network call.

## Tests

Synthetic in-memory outlines, edges, and joins. Cover a supersede that invalidates two edges and one citing join, a correct path that does the same, an outline with no edges, a join that cites a different revision, and a rank call that omits only the invalidated join. Assert the polygon and the join content bytes stay as they were. No TIFF, no project directory, and no `benchmarks/` read.

## Provenance effects

none. Invalidation appends a `StatusEvent`. It does not invent an envelope and does not change `provenance_id` on the edge or the join.
